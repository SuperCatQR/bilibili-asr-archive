# Content license boundaries

Project-owned original editor notes, explanations and editorial contributions are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This grant applies only to rights the contributor actually holds. It does not relicense source videos, audio, subtitles, transcripts of third-party expression, artwork or third-party data.

Project-owned factual metadata may be released under CC0-1.0 only when explicitly identified in a content notice. Third-party content keeps its original license and attribution. When compliance checks are enabled, unknown, revoked, expired or suspended permissions are excluded from public distribution.

Public exports under the compliance policy include `content-rights.json`, identifying each authorized edition's license, attribution, source and scope. Unchecked exports omit this notice. A license notice is not proof of copyright ownership: the operator must retain real permission evidence in the private rights ledger. Downloading, transcribing, AI proofreading or adding attribution does not create permission to relicense a third-party work.

The software source is GPL-3.0-only under the root LICENSE. This content policy is independent of the software license. Compliance workflow restrictions are disabled by default. Setting `BILI_COMPLIANCE_ENABLED=1` enables evidence checks and requires `BILI_PUBLICATION_ENABLED=1` for content-rights registration and public output. Disabling checks does not grant content permission; unchecked exports do not claim an open content license.
