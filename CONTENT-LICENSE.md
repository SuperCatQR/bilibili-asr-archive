# Content license boundaries

Project-owned original editor notes, explanations and editorial contributions are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This grant applies only to rights the contributor actually holds. It does not relicense source videos, audio, subtitles, transcripts of third-party expression, artwork or third-party data.

Project-owned factual metadata may be released under CC0-1.0 only when explicitly identified in a content notice. Third-party content keeps its original license and attribution. Unknown, revoked, expired or suspended permissions are private-only by default.

Public exports include `content-rights.json`, identifying each exact edition's license, attribution, source and authorized scope. A license notice is not proof of copyright ownership: the operator must retain real permission evidence in the private rights ledger. Downloading, transcribing, AI proofreading or adding attribution does not create permission to relicense a third-party work.

The software source is GPL-3.0-only under the root LICENSE. This content policy is independent of the software license. Content-rights registration and public-output commands are disabled unless the operator explicitly sets `BILI_PUBLICATION_ENABLED=1`; this setting does not grant any content permission.
