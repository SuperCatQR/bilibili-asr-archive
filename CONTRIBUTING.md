# Contributions and permissions

Submit original software contributions under GPL-3.0-only. Submit original nonsoftware editorial contributions under CC BY-SA 4.0, limited to rights you actually hold. Third-party components retain their original licenses.

By submitting a contribution, confirm that you wrote it or have sufficient permission to distribute it under its stated license. Identify copied material, its precise source, license, copyright notices and any required attribution. A Signed-off-by declaration documents your representation; it does not replace the rights holder's permission.

Do not submit real third-party transcripts, media, credentials, signed URLs, private permission correspondence or personal information as test fixtures. Use project-authored synthetic content for tests. Rights evidence remains in private storage, referenced by an opaque identifier and SHA-256.

Before distributing a bundled application or container, inspect the actual binary contents and provide the applicable original notices and corresponding source. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and docs/licensing-assessment.md.
